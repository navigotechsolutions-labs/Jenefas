# Jenefas Product Taxonomy & Catalog Specifications

This reference document structures Jenefas's hardware lineup for digital indexing, search filtering, and dealer specifications.

---

## 1. Product Categories & Standards

### Category 1: Fixed TV Wall Mounts (Slim Profile)
* **Target Audience:** Residential apartments, hotel rooms, budget installations.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * Profile distance to wall: 20 mm – 28 mm.
  * Material: Cold-rolled steel (1.5mm - 2.0mm gauge).
  * Built-in bubble level gauge.
* **Standard SKUs:**
  * `JF-FX32`: For 14" to 32" screens | VESA 75x75, 100x100, 200x200 | Max 25 kg
  * `JF-FX55`: For 32" to 55" screens | VESA up to 400x400 mm | Max 45 kg
  * `JF-FX75`: For 43" to 75" screens | VESA up to 600x400 mm | Max 60 kg
  * `JF-FX100`: Heavy Duty Commercial (65" - 100") | VESA up to 800x600 mm | Max 85 kg

---

### Category 2: Tilting TV Wall Mounts (Anti-Glare / Height Mount)
* **Target Audience:** Bedrooms (high mounting), conference rooms, hospital wards.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * Tilt range: 0° to -15° tool-less knob adjustment.
  * Wall clearance: 45 mm – 65 mm (allows clean HDMI/cable pass-through).
* **Standard SKUs:**
  * `JF-TL43`: 26" to 43" screens | Tilt -12° / +5° | Max 35 kg
  * `JF-TL65`: 32" to 65" screens | Tilt -15° / +5° | Max 50 kg
  * `JF-TL85`: 55" to 85" screens | Tilt -15° | Heavy Duty | Max 75 kg

---

### Category 3: Full-Motion Articulating Swivel Mounts
* **Target Audience:** Corner installations, open-plan living rooms, showroom displays.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * Dual-arm vs single-arm cantilever design.
  * Swivel: ±90° left-to-right | Tilt: ±15° | Level adjustment: ±3°.
  * Cable management clips built into arm.
* **Standard SKUs:**
  * `JF-FM32-S`: Single Arm Full Motion (17" - 42") | Extension: 380mm | Max 25 kg
  * `JF-FM55-D`: Dual Arm Heavy Duty (32" - 65") | Extension: 450mm | Max 45 kg
  * `JF-FM75-HD`: Extreme Heavy Duty Dual Arm (43" - 85") | Extension: 500mm | Max 65 kg
  * `JF-FM100-PRO`: Commercial Grade Double Arm (65" - 100") | Extension: 650mm | Max 90 kg

---

### Category 4: Ceiling & Commercial Display Mounts
* **Target Audience:** Showrooms, airports, restaurants, gymnasiums, educational institutes.
* **Standard SKUs:**
  * `JF-CL55-S`: Single TV Telescopic Ceiling Drop (32" - 55") | Drop: 600 - 1000mm | 360° Swivel
  * `JF-CL65-D`: Back-to-Back Dual Screen Ceiling Mount (40" - 65") | Drop: 1000 - 1500mm
  * `JF-VW-POP`: Video Wall Pop-Out Mount with Micro-Adjustment (46" - 65")

---

### Category 5: Desktop Ergonomics & Monitor Arms
* **Target Audience:** IT professionals, call centers, trading desks, home offices.
* **Standard SKUs:**
  * `JF-MA-01`: Gas-Spring Single Monitor Arm (Desk Clamp & Grommet, 17"-32", Max 9 kg)
  * `JF-MA-02`: Dual Screen Gas-Spring Monitor Arm (Independent 360° rotation)

## Limits and next review
- Frame sampling shows approximate changes over time, not continuous motion, turning direction or proof of tightening torque.
- Ask manufacturer to confirm the exact fastener list, placement, use of washers, tool sizes, tightening sequence and final checks before publishing a practical assembly guide.
- AI-generated fastener images are concepts and may invent hardware; they must not replace the source footage as evidence.
- For production animation, use close camera views for alignment and screw engagement; hold on each connection and show only verified movements after detailed footage review and manufacturer approval.

## 2. Plain-Language Talking Points for the Presentation

Use these talking points during the demonstration. Describe them as **intended business benefits**, not guaranteed sales metrics:

1. **Clear product pages help customers understand the range:**
   - Buyers can view the genuine finished product, archetype, and key dimensions before contacting the showroom.
2. **Fit information supports better-informed enquiries:**
   - Instead of asking generic questions ("Will this fit my TV?"), customers and dealers check their exact VESA spacing and display weight beforehand.
3. **Product context reduces repetitive sales questions:**
   - Answering whether a tabletop stand includes a rectangular base or angled V-base is solved visually in seconds, saving showroom staff valuable desk time.
4. **Approved assembly guidance can support customers and installers:**
   - Interactive 3D visualization reduces customer confusion during unboxing and post-purchase setup, helping AV installers and retail buyers understand how major sub-assemblies connect.

## Manufacturer review before instructional use

Confirm base geometry and wheel attachment; how the uprights and cross-member lock; the shelf support orientation and its fasteners; the plate attachment and rail engagement; the complete fastener list, tools and tightening requirements; and approved TV compatibility and attachment. The generated images are a visual storyboard, not photographic proof. The existing local 3D viewer and deployed viewer have not been updated to match these images.

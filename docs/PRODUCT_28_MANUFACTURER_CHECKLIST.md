## 5. Verified Assembly Sequence & Safety Instructions

Once the manufacturer verifies the physical items:

1. [ ] **Step 1:** Base preparation (apply anti-skid feet, align post collar).
2. [ ] **Step 2:** Fasten post to base plate (specify tightening torque, whether threadlocker is recommended).
3. [ ] **Step 3:** Mount horizontal rails to post (specify fixed position vs. adjustable height increments).
4. [ ] **Step 4:** TV bracket attachment & hang procedure (specify maximum TV screen weight in kg, maximum recommended screen size in inches, and center-of-gravity caution).
5. [ ] **Inspection check:** Verify perpendicular squareness (90° between post and base) before mounting the display.

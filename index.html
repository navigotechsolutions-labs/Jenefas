Industrial Strength Guarantee

Why Dealers Choose Jenefas Since 1978

Unlike flimsy imported brackets, Jenefas products are fabricated for Indian wall conditions and long-term safety.

Heavy-Gauge Cold Rolled Carbon Steel

Constructed with 1.5mm to 3.0mm thickness cold-rolled steel sheets. Tested to 3X to 4X rated load capacity without arm deflection or drooping over time.

Electrostatic Anti-Corrosion Coating

Every bracket undergoes automated ultrasonic degreasing and high-temperature matte black powder coating, resisting Chennai's coastal humidity and rust.

Complete Pro-Grade Hardware Kit

Every carton includes heavy Fischer-style nylon wall plugs, Grade 8.8 TV back screws (M4, M6, M8 with spacers), and integrated magnetic spirit levels.
